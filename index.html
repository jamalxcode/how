      What is How?

      How is a picture book that draws itself. Ask how a thing works — a combustion engine, scissors, a can opener, a zipper, a refrigerator, bicycle gears, why planes fly, how a house is built — and you get one exploded view with the parts numbered, a few short steps, a fun fact, photos of the real thing from Wikipedia and a 3D model from Sketchfab to spin. It explains in 17 languages, including Arabic, Urdu and Persian laid out right to left, and for three audiences: a curious adult, a 10-year-old or an engineering student.

      It is in the spirit of the old Tell Me Why books: almost no reading, one big picture. The words are written by Claude and the picture is painted by OpenAI's image model, both paid for through your own OpenRouter account (a few cents a page). Every answer in the gallery, and every answer someone shares with you, opens free with no sign-in. Made by Sala Company in Kuwait; the source is open.

      How is a free, static page: your OpenRouter account pays the AI; the pages you make are kept so they can be shared. Photos from Wikipedia, 3D models from Sketchfab (each with its own licence). · Source · Sala Company
